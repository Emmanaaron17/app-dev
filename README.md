## Main Characters

*   **Hiccup Horrendous Haddock III**
    *   An inventive, under-sized Viking teen who doesn't fit in with his dragon-slaying village.
    *   Befriends a rare Night Fury dragon named **Toothless**.
*   **Toothless**
    *   The last known *Night Fury*.
    *   Known as the "unholy offspring of lightning and death itself"—turns out to be more like a giant, scaly, playful cat.
*   **Astrid Hofferson**
    *   The fiercest teen warrior in the village.
    *   Initially skeptical of Hiccup, but later becomes his staunchest ally.

---

## Key Themes

1.  **Empathy over Fear:** Learning to understand what we don't know instead of automatically destroying it.
2.  **Redefining Strength:** Showing that intelligence, empathy, and innovation are just as valuable as brute physical force.
3.  **Found Family & Bonds:** The powerful partnership built between humans and animals.
